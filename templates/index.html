How CyberGuardian looks out for you

Every scan runs the same transparent pipeline: extract text and links from your image, look for behavioural red flags like urgency or credential requests, score the risk out of 100, and explain exactly why — in plain language, with clear next steps. Nothing is ever flagged as "guaranteed malicious." CyberGuardian shows you the evidence and lets you decide.

CyberGuardian AI flags potential phishing, scam and social-engineering indicators. It is not a guarantee of safety — always verify unexpected requests independently.
