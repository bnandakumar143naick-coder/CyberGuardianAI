Notification Shield

Android Notification Shield — real phone notifications analyzed by CyberGuardian AI

📱 How this works: This website does not have access to your phone. Notifications are captured by the CyberGuardian AI Android app (Android NotificationListenerService) and only after you explicitly grant Notification Access in Android Settings. The app sends the notification text to this server for analysis, and every scan appears below and in History.

Enable Notification Access: on the phone, open the CyberGuardian AI app → tap Enable Notification Access (opens Android Settings › Notification access) → switch CyberGuardian AI on.
